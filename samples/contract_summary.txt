Contract summary: office lease, Herzl Street 12, Tel Aviv

Parties: Green Leaf Design Ltd. (tenant) and Shalom Properties (landlord).
Term: 36 months starting 1 January 2027, with one option to extend by 24 months.
Rent: 9,500 ILS per month plus VAT, paid on the first of each month by bank transfer.
Deposit: a bank guarantee equal to three months of rent.

Documents attached to the signed contract:
1. Floor plan of the office (second floor, 120 square metres).
2. Insurance certificate for the tenant.
3. Signed guarantor form.

Maintenance: the landlord handles the building systems (elevator, air conditioning, water).
The tenant handles the interior. Any change to the walls needs written approval.

Documentation: keep copies of all invoices, payment confirmations and the exit inspection report.
All documents must be kept for seven years after the lease ends.

Exit: 90 days written notice. The office must be returned painted and clean.
